# Overview

The Italian (1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bc4) develops the bishop to its most active
square, eyeing the f7 pawn — the weakest point in Black's camp.[^wiki-italian]

# Main line

```
1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6 3. Bc4
```

ECO: C50. The Giuoco Piano (3...Bc5) and the Two Knights Defense (3...Nf6) are
the main replies.

# Strategic ideas

* White develops quickly and pressures f7, preparing c3 and d4 to build a
  strong center.
* Black equalizes by challenging the center with ...Nf6 and ...d5 or by
  mirroring with ...Bc5.
