# Overview

A fork is a single move that attacks two or more enemy pieces simultaneously,
forcing the opponent to save one and lose the other.[^wiki-fork] The knight is
the most feared forking piece because its L-shaped move is easy to overlook.

# Recognition

* Scan the opponent's king, queen, and rooks for squares where one of your
  pieces attacks two of them at once.
* A *royal fork* attacks the king and another piece, so it wins material with
  tempo.

# Training

Solve fork-themed puzzles, then look for fork opportunities in your own games
after every knight and pawn move.

[^wiki-fork]: Definition and examples per the linked Wikipedia article.
