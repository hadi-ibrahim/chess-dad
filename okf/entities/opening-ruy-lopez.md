# Overview

The Ruy Lopez (1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bb5) pins the knight that defends e5,
building long-term pressure against Black's center rather than seeking an
immediate attack.[^wiki-ruy]

# Main line

```
1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6 3. Bb5 a6 4. Ba4 Nf6 5. O-O
```

ECO: C60. The Berlin (3...Nf6) and the Morphy (3...a6) are the main branches.

# Strategic ideas

* White maintains the tension and aims to build a strong pawn center with c3
  and d4.
* Black seeks counterplay via ...a6 and ...b5, and often ...Nf6 challenging e4.
