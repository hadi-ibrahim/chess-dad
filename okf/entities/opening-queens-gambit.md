# Overview

The Queen's Gambit (1.d4 d5 2.c4) offers a wing pawn to undermine Black's
central d5-pawn. If Black accepts and tries to keep the pawn, White regains it
with advantage; declining (2...e6 or 2...c6) is more solid.[^wiki-qg]

# Main line

```
1. d4 d5 2. c4
```

ECO: D06. The Queen's Gambit Declined (2...e6, D30) and the Slav (2...c6, D10)
are the main continuations.

# Strategic ideas

* White fights for the center and the c-file while developing naturally.
* Black holds the d5 point and prepares ...e6 and ...c6 to consolidate before
  striking back.
