# Overview

The Sicilian (1.e4 c5) is Black's most popular and combative reply to the King's
Pawn. By playing ...c5, Black trades a flank pawn for White's central d-pawn and
avoids symmetry, creating unbalanced positions rich in winning chances for both
sides.[^wiki-sicilian]

# Main line

```
1. e4 c5 2. Nf3 d6 3. d4 cxd4 4. Nxd4 Nf6 5. Nc3
```

ECO: B20. The Najdorf (5...a6, ECO B90) is the most celebrated branch.

# Strategic ideas

* Black accepts a space disadvantage in exchange for long-term counterplay on
  the half-open c-file and against White's center.
* White plays for a kingside attack; Black plays for queenside counterplay and
  the d5 break.
